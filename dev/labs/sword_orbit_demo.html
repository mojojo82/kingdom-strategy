Orbiting Swords

Test: ghost-energy broadswords (art from ChatGPT: one face view + one edge view) circle a code-drawn hero on a tilted ring. Each sword turns as it goes round: wide when its face points at you, thin at the sides where it is seen edge-on. On the far half of the ring they are drawn before the hero (behind), on the near half after (in front), and they shrink and dim slightly with distance.

Real Conquest screen at true phone size (same pixels as the game). The sword hero is Gareth's height, next to him.

How it would work in the game: the hero stays an Atlas sprite (or any animation); only the swords are code. Each frame the swords are split by depth: the far ones draw just before the hero sprite, the near ones just after. The game already layers effects behind and in front of heroes this way, so it works the same in Conquest and Arena.
