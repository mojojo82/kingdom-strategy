Orbiting Swords

The new hero at real game size in Arena: his Atlas idle (the hover bob is code), green energy swords orbiting him, and his skill. Skill: the swords fly skyward, a giant energy sword drops onto the enemy ship and sticks in the deck, dissolves, and the swords warp back in.

How it would work in the game: the hero is his Atlas idle sheet; the orbit, the flying swords, the giant sword, the impact and the dissolve are all code drawing a few pictures, so it works the same in Conquest and Arena. Close-up zooms the camera on him to inspect the swords.
