On the phone this is pinch zoom; the levels blend into each other instead of switching.

What each zoom shows

🌍 World
Every kingdom as one shape on the world grid with its name, player count and the alliances holding the most ground (coloured areas). Your kingdom has the gold edge. One small summary per kingdom, refreshed every few minutes, so it costs the same with 2 kingdoms or 100.
🗺️ Middle
Bases become small dots in their alliance colour (yours cyan), province lines and names stay. Loads the patch lists only, no march detail. The neighbour kingdom's dots show across the border the same way.
🏰 Close
The normal game view. Panning over the dotted border shows the next kingdom's land, resources, bases and marches exactly like yours, with a short "Entering Vanaheim" banner. Tapping something over there shows its info; attack, teleport, rally and gather are greyed out while borders are closed.
