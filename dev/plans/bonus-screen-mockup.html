How it works
Each lane adds a flat amount to the game's global ATK / HP / DEF Bonus (the same number Equipment adds to). The cost per level rises slowly. Every 10 levels in all three lanes gives an extra bonus to all three plus a reward, so spreading out beats dumping everything into one lane.
Placeholder numbers in this mockup
+1% per level in each lane. Cost = 10 + 4 × level. Milestones every 10 levels: +5% to all three, plus a chest. Real values come from the balance sims, including your point that HP tends to be the strongest lane.
