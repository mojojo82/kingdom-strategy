Seamless World Map Plan

11 Oct 2026 · not built yet

Goal

One continuous world: a player can zoom out from their base, fly over every kingdom, and zoom back in anywhere. No loading screens, and no sense of changing kingdom except the border line and the name.

  • Zoomed in (normal play): real bases, marches and resources for the area on screen.
  • Zoomed out (exploring): one summary per kingdom: name, player count, which alliances hold which areas.
  • Cost and phone load depend on what is on screen, not on how many players a kingdom has. 100 kingdoms of 1,000+ players costs each phone about the same as one.

How it works today

Drawing already scales; the shared data does not. Every player downloads, and live-listens to, their whole kingdom's bases and marches.

Rough cost: 10,000 march updates a day × 1,000 watching players = 10 million Firebase reads a day for one kingdom. Across 100 kingdoms that's likely hundreds of dollars a day. Not a problem at today's player count; it needs fixing before a kingdom passes a couple of hundred active players.

The design

The shared data gets organised into map patches, and each phone listens only to the patches on screen, the same way land and sea already load.

World atlas. One small shared list of every kingdom and its place on a world grid (Atlantis at 0,0, the test kingdom beside it). Each kingdom keeps its own map and data; the atlas only says where to draw it.

Patches. Each kingdom's map is cut into 50 × 50 tile patches (a 1,200 × 1,200 kingdom = 576 patches). Each patch has one shared list of the bases in it: owner, name, alliance tag, skin, HP and burn state.

What each phone listens to:

  • The patches on screen plus one ring around them (about 9 to 16 at normal zoom), across kingdom borders.
  • Marches whose route touches those patches.
  • Always, wherever they are: your own marches, your alliance's, and anything attacking you.
  • Patches you scroll away from stop updating but stay in memory, so scrolling back is instant.

Between levels, the closer detail fades in instead of popping. Kingdom summaries are refreshed by a server job every few minutes. Panning into a neighbour loads its patches exactly like your own.

The test kingdom

A real second kingdom sits next to Atlantis, replacing the drawn "Kingdom 1" placeholder. Play testers live there.

  • Its own everything: own map, bases and marches, so nothing testers do touches Atlantis players.
  • Getting testers in: an admin tool to move an account in (and back), and optionally an invite code for brand-new accounts. Normal sign-ups keep going to Atlantis.
  • Fake crowd for load tests: a script fills it with 1,000 bot bases and a few hundred marches. This runs on the local fake server until you OK writing them to the TEST Firebase.

Build order

Everything is built and proven on the test kingdom first. Atlantis players only see a change in phase 5.

1 · Test kingdom and a baseline
Real second kingdom next to Atlantis, admin tool to move testers in, script for a 1,000-bot crowd.
Measure today's setup with the crowd: Firebase reads per player, phone time per update.
Gate: baseline numbers written down
2 · Bases by patch
Patch lists kept in step with bases; phones listen only to patches on screen plus one ring.
Old whole-kingdom list stays behind a switch. Test: same bases visible, reads compared to baseline.
Gate: no base missing or doubled across 1,000 bots, reads well down
3 · Marches by patch
Each march records the patches it crosses; phones load the marches touching their view.
Own, alliance and incoming marches always live. Test: no attack goes unseen.
Gate: every attack seen in scripted tests
4 · World view and crossing kingdoms
World atlas, kingdom summaries, three zoom levels with fade-in, panning into the neighbour.
Test: you pan and zoom across both kingdoms on your phone; fps and reads measured again.
Gate: your OK on the feel
5 · Move Atlantis over
Copy Atlantis's bases and marches into patches, switch on, keep the old way for rollback.
Then remove the placeholder outline and the old whole-list code.

Decisions for you

  • Test kingdom name
  • Which side of Atlantis it sits on
  • How testers get in: admin moves them, an invite code, or both
  • Cross-kingdom actions: look only to start, or attack / teleport / rally across borders
  • Bot bases: cleared before testers arrive, or kept for a busier map
  • OK to write the fake 1,000-player crowd into the TEST Firebase
  • Patch size: 50 × 50 tiles proposed
